## Look-alikes and inflations
- A "Phantom" digital agency ≠ Phantom wallet. "Polygon.io" ≠ Polygon. Jump Trading ≠ Jump Crypto.
- Ambassador / DevRel work-group / community roles are peripheral, not employment.
- Agency engagements listed as jobs; "<brand> via <staffing firm>"; "(contract)" — discount these.

## Fraud tells
- **Identity mismatch:** handles/emails unrelated to the name; a LinkedIn slug or CV that belongs to someone else.
- **Missing trail:** no LinkedIn, no employers, no dates, empty CV, chat-app-only contact.
- **Geography doesn't fit:** name, school, phone and city point to different countries; placeholder phone numbers.
- **Too broad:** 30–50 skills, many job categories, "senior dev on 6+ chains".
- **Borrowed credibility:** real brands with impossible timelines; every job a top brand, all remote.
- **Text artifacts:** template phrases, AI chat replies inside the CV, CVs tailored to another employer.
- **Numbers:** round too-good metrics, years claimed > CV dates, credentials impossible for the tenure.
- **Pay:** very low ask for the claimed seniority, or an extreme ask on a thin profile.
- **Not trust signals on their own:** community membership, hackathon participation, a polished CV.

## Data traps
- "Years" fields are often wrong — use CV dates.
- AI scores often copy the human score.
- Duplicate records with different data — evaluate the person once and flag the merge.
